Terms Of Service

Will Do

Will Not Do

  • Dragons and Mythical/Combined Beasts
  • Animals/Creatures/Monsters
  • Light to Medium Gore
  • Humanoids (no human faces, please ask!)
  • NSFW
  • Excessive Gore
  • Illegal or Highly Political Works
  • Anything that makes me uncomfortable

I’m willing to experiment with other subjects such as mechas and weapons, but may take longer.

I can refuse to do a commission for any reason. Attempting to force me to draw anything that makes me uncomfortable or that is in my "Will Not Do" list will result in me blocking the account.

Revision/Editing Periods

Period 1 --- between sketch phase to ink phase (NOT including ink/line art)

Period 2 --- During coloring of base colors

Period 3 --- After full completion (minor touchups)

Each period is allowed up to 3 edits

Up to 3 large edits before Inking/Line art.

Any major edits during the coloring phase that do not relate to color will incur extra charges.

Any major edits after completion will be subject to my approval and incur extra charges.

Turnaround Time

3 Days to Two Weeks

Usually a piece will take me a few days/under two weeks to complete.

I will do my best to notify the client of any delays or complications during the process.

Payment

All payments will be made either 100% upfront or 50% upfront and 50% after completion.

Payments will be taken through PayPal, Kofi, or Vgen.

No Refunds after the project has been started. All refunds are subject to my approval.

Artwork that is highly complex or detailed will incur extra charges.

If the artwork needs to be completed by a certain deadline, please notify me before I accept the commission as this may incur an extra charge depending on the date.

Refund Policy

NO REFUNDS

Any refunds are entirely subject to me and may vary depending on circumstance.

If I am unable to complete a commission for whatever reason, I may issue a refund that seems reasonable for the amount of effort/completion the piece has reached. I will send the current progress and line art(if applicable) regardless.

ABSOLUTELY NO REFUNDS AFTER INK PHASE!

With both traditional and digital line art I must pay for replacement/maintenance of my materials and be compensated for time spent.

Thank you for your understanding.

Communication

I try my best to answer any inquiries as soon as possible, however due to possible time difference please expect a response from me within 2-3 days of messaging me.

If I send an inquiry to the client and do not hear from them for more than two weeks from the original message, the commission is considered forfeit.

Rude or disrespectful clients will be ignored/blocked.
